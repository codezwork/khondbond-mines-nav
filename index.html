Welcome to Khondbond Iron Mine

Khondbond Iron and Manganese Mine is a vital facility. Use this digital guide to navigate the premises safely.

General Safety Instructions
  1. PPEs are required at all operational sites within the mine.
  2. Ensure only full-sleeve clothing is worn, as half-sleeve garments are not permitted inside the mine premises.
  3. Always remain accompanied by authorized mine personnel during the visit.
  4. Follow all mine traffic rules, speed limits, warning signage and instructions from safety personnel.
  5. Stay clear of operating equipment, dumpers, excavators, drilling, blasting areas etc.
  6. Blasting activity is scheduled between 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM. Entry into danger zones during blasting hours is strictly prohibited.
  7. Vehicle entry into operational areas is permitted only after approval from the Mine Manager / authorized personnel.
  8. Use of mobile phones, photography and videography inside mine premises is restricted and requires prior permission from the Mine Manager/ authorized personnel.
  9. Re-use/Re-fill of plastic water bottles for drinking purpose inside the mine premises is prohibited.
  10. Do not walk close to pit edges, benches, haul roads or restricted areas.
  11. Report any unsafe condition, incident or emergency immediately to the accompanying official.
  12. In case of emergency, immediately contact: 8093754800.
